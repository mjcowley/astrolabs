The C3 proforma

Overview

In this AstroLab you'll compose Red-Green-Blue (RGB) composite images from astronomical data. In Part 1, you'll build an image of the Cosmic Cliffs in the Carina Nebula from three James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) images. In Part 2, you'll research an object called NGC 7293, build your own RGB image of it from three very different infrared wavelengths, and work out what the colours are telling you.

By the end of this AstroLab you'll be able to:

  • open and display FITS images in SAOImageDS9
  • adjust an image's contrast so faint detail shows up
  • read a filter throughput plot
  • combine three images into an RGB composite and explain your colour choices
  • research an astronomical object, and explain what different wavebands reveal about it

How to complete this AstroLab

You can complete C3 on campus in your scheduled session, or remotely in your own time. The steps are the same either way, and everyone submits the same proforma.

On campus

  • Come to your scheduled AstroLab session. SAOImageDS9 is already installed on the lab computers.
  • The teaching team will run a live demonstration and help you as you go.

Remote

  • Watch the walkthrough recording on Echo360. It covers everything from the live session.
  • Install SAOImageDS9 on your own computer, or use QUT's Virtual Desktop Infrastructure.
  • Work through Before you start on the GitHub page first, so setup problems don't hold you up.
  • If you get stuck, contact the teaching team.

Finished? Submit your completed proforma on the Assignments page.
