The C3 proforma

Overview

In this AstroLab you'll use TOPCAT to download measurements of the stars near the Sun from the European Space Agency's Gaia space telescope, turn them into your own Hertzsprung–Russell (H–R) diagram, clean out the unreliable measurements, and explore what the diagram tells us about how stars live and die. In Part 2, you'll find the Hyades, a cluster of stars that were all born together, from the way its stars move across the sky, and see how its H–R diagram reveals the cluster's age.

By the end of this AstroLab you'll be able to:

  • query a professional astronomy archive and work with a large table of stars in TOPCAT
  • build an H–R diagram from parallaxes, magnitudes and colours
  • explain why astronomers remove unreliable measurements, and how
  • identify the main sequence, giants and white dwarfs, and explain what makes each group different
  • find a star cluster from the motions of its stars, and explain what its H–R diagram tells us about its age

How to complete this AstroLab

You can complete C3 on campus in your scheduled session, or remotely in your own time. The steps are the same either way, and everyone submits the same proforma.

On campus

  • Come to your scheduled AstroLab session. TOPCAT is already installed on the lab computers.
  • The teaching team will run a background information session and help you as you go.

Remote

  • Watch the recording of the background information session on Echo360. It covers everything from the live session.
  • Install TOPCAT on your own computer, or use QUT's Virtual Desktop Infrastructure.
  • Work through Before you start on the GitHub page first, so setup problems don't hold you up.
  • If you get stuck, contact the teaching team.

Finished? Submit your completed proforma on the Assignments page.
